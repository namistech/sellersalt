# SellerSalt — Shop Health & Optimization Specification

- **Document Version:** 2.0.0
- **Status:** Canonical Specification
- **System Classification:** Store-Level Health Auditing & Growth Action Roadmaps

---

## 1. Executive Purpose

**Shop Optimization** (`/shop-health`) provides connected store owners with a comprehensive, prioritized diagnostic audit of their overall Etsy shop configuration, catalog health, and growth opportunities.

---

## 2. The 6 Dimensions of Shop Health

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        SHOP HEALTH AUDIT DIMENSIONS                         │
├──────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. CATALOG SEO COMPLETENESS (30%)    │ 2. LISTING DIVERSITY & SIZE (20%)    │
│    • % of listings using all 13 tags │    • Active catalog size vs niche avg│
│    • % of listings with titles >120ch│    • Price tier diversity (Entry/Mid)│
├──────────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. CONVERSION & LISTING YIELD (20%)  │ 4. SHOP BRANDING & POLICIES (10%)    │
│    • Active listings with zero sales │    • Shop banner, icon & about video │
│    • High-traffic low-converting items│   • Return & shipping policies set  │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 5. REVIEW VELOCITY & RATING (10%)    │ 6. RENEWAL & INVENTORY HYGIENE (10%) │
│    • Review average (Target: ≥4.8★)  │    • Expiring / stale draft clean-up │
│    • Recent 30-day review momentum   │    • Out-of-stock variations alert   │
└──────────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 3. Prioritized Shop Growth Action Plan

The audit generates a prioritized checklist of actionable tasks pushed directly into the **Planner**:
1. **Critical Tasks**: Fix 4 listings with fewer than 10 tags.
2. **Growth Tasks**: Expand top-selling digital planner into a physical spiral notebook.
3. **Hygiene Tasks**: Archive 6 listings that have had zero favorites and zero sales in 12 months.
