# SellerSalt — Canonical Product Positioning

Authoritative product definition, core promise, workflow, and platform positioning.

---

## 1. Product Identity

**SellerSalt is the Ecommerce Intelligence Platform.**

Core Promise: **"Know what to sell before you spend money."**

SellerSalt empowers independent ecommerce merchants, agencies, and brand builders to discover high-potential product opportunities, research market demand and competition, validate commercial viability, design differentiated product configurations, model financial unit economics, and prepare actionable launch plans.

## 3. What SellerSalt Is NOT

To protect platform integrity, compliance, and user trust:

- **SellerSalt is NOT a marketplace scraper.** It does not use stealth botting, browser fingerprint spoofing, or CAPTCHA circumvention.
- **SellerSalt is NOT an API bypass tool.** It does not attempt to circumvent marketplace rate limits or access controls.
- **SellerSalt is NOT an Etsy or Amazon replacement.** Marketplaces are distribution channels and public data sources; SellerSalt's intelligence layer is the product.
- **SellerSalt is NOT a clone of marketplace databases.** It observes aggregate signals and synthesizes derived strategies.

---

## 4. Multi-Source Intelligence Architecture

SellerSalt integrates multiple legitimate acquisition channels:
1. **Public Web Research (Primary)**: Standardized HTML/JSON-LD parsing of publicly accessible product listings and search results where permitted by policy.
2. **Authorized Marketplace APIs (Secondary)**: Official APIs (e.g. Etsy Open API v3, Amazon SP-API, eBay REST) for structured catalog queries and enriched confidence.
3. **Connected Merchant Stores**: OAuth-authorized store data for inventory, order syncing, and own-shop operations.
4. **Historical Market Memory (Tertiary)**: Longitudinal trend tracking and snapshot memory built from SellerSalt's own historical observations.
5. **User-Supplied Sourcing Inputs**: Landed manufacturing costs, supplier quotes, packaging fees, and target CAC entered directly by merchants.
