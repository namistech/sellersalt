# SellerSalt Brand Copy Guidelines & Trademark Disclosures

## Words and framings to never use
spy, spying, surveillance, surveil, stalk, track (as in "track competitors" — "track your own listings" is fine), hack, exploit, bypass, circumvent, scrape, scraping, crawl, crawler, covert, secret, hidden (in the context of watching other sellers), "see what your competitors don't want you to see."

## Words and framings that are fine
research, discover, benchmark, analyze, evidence-based, data-driven, market intelligence, competitive research, observed, estimated, labeled, transparent, verified, "manage your ecommerce business with your own AI agent," "connect your AI assistant via MCP," optimize, audit, plan, launch.

## Review checklist before publishing any new copy
- No banned word appears anywhere, including alt text, meta descriptions, code comments visible in dev tools
- Any marketplace name mentioned has the trademark disclaimer present on that page
- No claim implies access to another seller's private/non-public shop data
- AI-related claims are scoped to the Assistant/MCP features specifically, not the core research/scoring engine
- Data referenced in copy matches what the Zero-Fabrication Contract would actually label as Observed
