# SellerSalt Market Radar 2.0 & Decision Intelligence

Authoritative guide to **Opportunity Radar 2.0**, SellerSalt's continuous multi-marketplace decision intelligence feed.

---

## 1. Overview

Market Radar 2.0 provides an autonomous feed of high-velocity niches, lean catalogs, price gaps, and emerging products discovered across Etsy, Amazon, eBay, and Walmart.

---

## 2. Categorized Opportunity Sections

1. **Emerging Opportunities**:
   - Freshly observed listings and keywords gaining initial market visibility.
2. **Rising Momentum**:
   - Products with high positive review velocity ($\ge 0.5$ reviews/day) and expanding keyword prevalence.
3. **Underserved Attributes & Niches**:
   - High relevance search terms with low listing saturation.
4. **Price Gaps & Margin Windows**:
   - Clear price tier opportunities above standard budget clusters.
5. **Cross-Marketplace Expansion**:
   - Products validated on one marketplace with multi-channel potential.
6. **Differentiation Gaps**:
   - Products with distinctive materials or styles against generic incumbents.
7. **Persistent Leaders**:
   - Stable opportunities verified across $\ge 7$ days of tracking.

---

## 3. Real-Time Pulse Metrics

Radar 2.0 summarizes:
- Total opportunities discovered
- Emerging, Rising, Underserved, and Price Gap counts
- Average opportunity composite score
- Top 5 dominant product categories with average scores
